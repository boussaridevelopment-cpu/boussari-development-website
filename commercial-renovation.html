How larger commercial jobs run

One coordinator, start to close

Assess and price it

We walk the space with you, review the scope and the budget, and come back with a clear written proposal.

Drawings, permits, trades

We prepare the drawings, apply for the permits, coordinate engineering when required, and schedule the licensed trades.

Build, inspect, close out

One project manager supervises the site, arranges the inspections, and walks you through the finished space at handover.

Coordination Note Engineering, electrical, plumbing, HVAC and other regulated work is coordinated with licensed professionals and licensed trades when required. Boussari Development acts as your single point of project management and coordination.
