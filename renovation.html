Why it works differently

Renovation With a Real Estate Lens

Our leadership works the Ontario real-estate market every day, so renovation advice can go beyond looks. When we suggest a change, we weigh what buyers in that neighborhood expect, what renters will pay, what it costs, and how the space will hold up in use.

We help you prioritize the improvements that make sense for the property, and we never promise a specific resale value or return.

Whether it is a door, a floor, a bathroom or a whole house, we ask the same question first. What does the property need, and how much should you spend on it?
