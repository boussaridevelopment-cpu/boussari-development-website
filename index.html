01 · Home Renovation & Improvement

Renovation With a Real Estate Lens

Our leadership includes active Ontario real-estate market experience. That means renovation recommendations can consider more than appearance: neighborhood standards, buyer expectations, rental appeal, marketability, functionality, budget and long-term use can all be part of the conversation.

The goal is to help clients prioritize improvements that make sense for the property — without promising a specific resale value or return. From flooring, doors and bathrooms to kitchens, basements and full-home renovations, small projects are welcome and larger projects can be coordinated from scope and budgeting through construction, finishing and completion.
