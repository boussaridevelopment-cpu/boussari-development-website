Our Story

One Simple Idea

Boussari Development was built around a simple idea: property decisions are stronger when construction, real estate and project strategy are connected.

Our background combines hands-on renovation experience, project coordination and active real-estate market knowledge. That allows us to help a homeowner decide what to improve, help an investor understand what a property may need, and manage the practical work required to move the property toward the client's objective. From a focused home improvement to a legal income suite, commercial project or value-add renovation, our role is to organize the right path and coordinate the project through completion.
