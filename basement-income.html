Financing Coordination

We Connect the Financing to the Project

Eligible homeowners can explore home-equity / HELOC financing through qualified mortgage or lending partners. We help coordinate the financing process alongside the project scope.

Once financing is approved and funds become available according to the lender's terms, the construction schedule and payment stages can be coordinated with the project — so the homeowner is not left trying to organize financing and renovation separately. HELOC rates are variable, at the lender's prime plus a margin. You qualify under the federal stress test, so your real payment is confirmed at approval. Federal rules cap a HELOC at 65% of the home's value, or 80% combined with a mortgage.

  • Mortgage-broker / lending-partner coordination for eligible projects
  • HELOC / home-equity financing options
  • Budget-to-financing planning before construction
  • Coordination of project scope and payment stages with approved financing terms
  • Project-cost and payment scenario discussion based on qualification and current lender terms
