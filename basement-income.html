The process, end to end

Plan your basement conversion

You do not have to arrange the financing first, then find a designer, permit specialist, contractor and leasing help on your own. Boussari Development coordinates the process as one connected project.

We review the property and project goals, help arrange suitable HELOC or home-equity financing through licensed mortgage brokers, organize design and building-permit work, manage construction and code inspections, and prepare the completed legal suite for the rental market.

We run the financing and the build as one job, on one schedule.

Financing Coordination

Explore financing options

Eligible homeowners can explore home-equity / HELOC financing through qualified mortgage or lending partners. We help coordinate the financing process alongside the project scope.

Once financing is approved and funds become available according to the lender's terms, the construction schedule and payment stages can be coordinated with the project — so the homeowner is not left trying to organize financing and renovation separately. A licensed mortgage broker or lender can explain available products, eligibility, repayment terms and current rates for your circumstances.

  • Mortgage-broker / lending-partner coordination for eligible projects
  • HELOC / home-equity financing options
  • Budget-to-financing planning before construction
  • Coordination of project scope and payment stages with approved financing terms
  • Project-cost and payment scenario discussion based on qualification and current lender terms

Plan the numbers

Understand the costs before you build

A useful rental plan starts with your property, the proposed work and local rental comparisons. Review the renovation budget and ongoing expenses alongside any financing payments before deciding how to proceed.

  • Construction, design and permit costs based on the assessed scope
  • Financing payments and repayment terms confirmed by your lender
  • Rental estimates based on comparable local properties and the proposed suite
  • Operating costs, maintenance and an allowance for vacancy
Financing and rental estimates Financing is subject to lender approval, available home equity, credit qualification and product terms.
